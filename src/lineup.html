Where this comes from

Artist names are read from the official Treefort lineup page. Spotify links come from the festival's own performer pages where they have one, otherwise from a Spotify search by name. Apple Music, Tidal and Bandcamp links come from MusicBrainz and the festival; where we have none, the link is a search on that service. Similar artists and genre tags come from Last.fm. Some matches are corrected by hand.

Artists marked unverified have a Spotify match we could not confirm (a near or shared name), so their Spotify link may point to a different act.

Artist links from MusicBrainz (CC0). Similar-artist data and tags from Last.fm.
