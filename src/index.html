        Connect your Spotify library

        Spotifort can read your Liked Songs, top artists and followed artists and add them to My artists. Because of Spotify's rules this needs Spotify Premium and a one-time setup: you create your own free Spotify developer app and paste its Client ID here. It takes about five minutes on a computer.

        Setup guide · Why is this needed?

        32 letters and numbers, from your app's settings on the Spotify developer dashboard.

        Only your Client ID can be remembered, never your Spotify login. The access token stays in this tab's memory and is gone when you close it.

        For best results, open this site in Safari or Chrome, not an in-app browser.
